They told you: pick one of three boxes. We're telling you all three are rigged against the people who get in them. Read it once and decide.

The college trap

Average debt: $37K. Average starting salary: $55K. You graduate at 22 carrying a car payment that buys you 3 nothing. Then you spend the next decade catching up to where you'd be without it.

The 9-to-5 ceiling

You trade your hours for the smallest number a manager can sign off on. Raise once a year if you behave. Promotion goes to someone older. Your ceiling is what HR allows.

The door-to-door grind

You learn to sell. Good. But alarms, solar, pest — they pay you for the close and keep the renewal forever. You start over Monday. The math never compounds for you.

Here's what the next 12 months actually look like.

Not a roadmap. Not a 30-page producer agreement. The real cadence — what we ask of you, what we give you, and what the math turns into if you show up.

Week 1

You apply, we interview, we start your license

20-minute call. If we're a fit, you start state pre-license study that night — we cover materials, send you a study buddy, and we don't take a dime until you write your first policy.

Wk 3–6

You pass life & health, get appointed at top carriers

Our back-office handles the contracting paperwork at 30+ A-rated carriers. You don't fight the portals — you study the products.

Month 2

First dials on inbound leads. Live-call coaching from day one.

You're not knocking doors. You're calling families who asked us about coverage. A mentor sits the first 50 dials with you on a 3-way line, then debriefs every one. This is the part you can't buy on YouTube.

Month 3

First $5K month

What an active producer who runs the dials hits. Some run $10K. Almost none who run the activity run zero. (Activity = 20 hours/wk on the dialer minimum, no exceptions.)

Month 6

Consistent $8–15K months + renewals stacking

You're closing without the mentor on the line. Your renewals from month 3 onwards start showing up. You realize the math is finally compounding for you — not your manager.

Yr 1

$80–150K take-home for the half that actually run the system

Top quartile producers cross $200K. The bottom quartile quit because they wouldn't sit on the dialer. We can't fix that. If you'll do the work, the system pays.

Yr 2–3

Start your team. Override + renewals = real residual income.

You learn to recruit the version of you from a year ago. You earn an override on every policy your downline writes. Your renewals from year 1 and 2 keep paying. This is when the income stops being linear.
