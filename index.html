Our approach

Three principles. No quotas.

01

Independent by design

We're not captive to any single carrier. That means we can place you with whichever insurer underwrites your situation best — not the one paying us most.

02

Education before product

You'll never hear a pitch until we've understood what you're protecting. Term, whole, IUL, annuity — each fits a specific stage and goal. We'll show you the math.

03

Built to hold for life

The right policy is the one your family still has 30 years from now. We optimize for persistency — not premium spike — so the protection actually pays out.

What we place

Coverage for every stage of life.

Term Life

Pure protection. Highest coverage per dollar.

  • 10, 20, or 30-year level premiums
  • Income replacement for young families
  • Mortgage and debt protection
  • Convertible to permanent later

Whole Life

Lifetime coverage with guaranteed cash value.

  • Premiums never increase
  • Cash value compounds tax-deferred
  • Borrow against your policy
  • Generational wealth transfer

IUL

Indexed universal life — flexibility plus upside.

  • Cash value linked to market index
  • 0% floor — never lose principal to market drops
  • Tax-advantaged retirement income
  • Adjustable premium & death benefit

Final Expense

Burial & end-of-life protection. Simplified issue.

  • $5K – $50K coverage amounts
  • Most plans: no medical exam
  • Locked-in rates
  • Available up to age 85

Annuities

Guaranteed income for retirement.

  • Single-premium & multi-year guarantees
  • Indexed annuities for upside with floor
  • Lifetime income riders
  • Tax-deferred growth

Mortgage Protection

A specialized term policy tied to your loan.

  • Decreasing or level term options
  • Living benefits available
  • Return-of-premium riders
  • Disability + critical illness add-ons
